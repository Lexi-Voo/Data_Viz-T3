# Data visualisation T03

## Data Story  
This project explores the Australian television market, focusing on screen technologies, sizes, brands, and energy efficiency.

**Audience**  
- Australian households buying a TV  
- Individuals concerned with energy costs  
- People interested in popular TV models  

**Purpose**  
The story highlights which TVs are most common, how size affects energy use, and which technologies are most efficient. Overall, **55-inch LCD TVs stand out as the best balance of popularity, brand choice, and energy efficiency**.  

---

## About the Data  
- **Source**: Australian Government Energy Rating Database.  
- **Processing**: Filtered to available TVs in Australia, remove duplicated Model_No rows, dropping columns with empty value, cleaned brand/technology names by convert all to uppercase  
- **Privacy**: No personal or sensitive data, only product details.  
- **Accuracy & Limitations**: Covers only registered models; real household usage may differ.  
- **Ethics**: Data is used responsibly to promote sustainable consumer choices.
