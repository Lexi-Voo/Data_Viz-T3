Television Energy Insights

This section explores the Australian television market, focusing on screen technologies, sizes, brands, and energy efficiency. The goal is to help consumers make informed decisions based on energy usage and popularity of different TVs.

Target Audience

  • Australian households considering purchasing a TV
  • Individuals who care about energy efficiency and electricity costs
  • People interested in popular TV models and brands

What types of TVs are sold in Australia?

LCD(LED) are by far the most commonly sold TVs in Australia, followed by LCD and OLED.

What TV sizes are most popular?

55-inch TVs is the most popular choice for Australian households.

Which brand has the most models?

SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS have the most variety of TV models, followed by KOGAN and LG.

Which TV technology uses the least energy?

LCD TVs consumed the least power, while OLED TVs consumed the most energy.

Do bigger TVs use more energy?

Yes, larger TVs generally use more electricity compared to smaller ones.

Do star ratings depend on screen size?

Not really, star ratings are spread across all TV sizes. Bigger TVs don't necessarily mean worse efficiency.

Conclusion

Energy efficiency is key for reducing electricity consumption. Australian households can benefit from selecting 55-inch LCD TVs, which balance screen size, popularity, brand variety, and lower power usage.
